# Product Brief: VibeCode Community

---

## Product: **VibeCode** *(working name)*

## Audience:
AI-assisted coders, "vibe coders", and creative developers who use AI tools (like ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, AgnesCode) to build projects but struggle to find a focused community to share work, get feedback, and learn from peers.

## Problem:
Vibe coders lack a centralized space to:
- Showcase what they've built with AI
- Get constructive feedback on their AI-generated projects
- Learn prompt engineering and workflow tips from others
- Connect with like-minded creators who "code by vibe" not just logic

Existing platforms (GitHub, Discord, Reddit) are either too technical, too fragmented, or lack the casual creative energy of vibe coding culture.

## Promise:
Easier to **discover**, **learn**, and **connect** with other vibe coders in one vibrant, visual-first community hub.

## Core Journey:
`Discover` → `Join a Tribe` → `Browse & Learn` → `Share Your Vibe` → `Get Feedback & Grow`

1. **Discover** — Land on homepage, see featured projects and trending vibes
2. **Join a Tribe** — Browse communities (Web Dev, AI Tools, Games, Design, etc.)
3. **Browse & Learn** — Scroll through posts, tutorials, prompts, and project showcases
4. **Share Your Vibe** — Post your project, prompt, or creation with tags
5. **Get Feedback & Grow** — Comment, like, save, and follow other creators

## Must Have (MVP Features):
1. **Community/Tribe Pages** — Browse and join interest-based groups (e.g., "AI Web Apps", "Retro Games", "Prompt Engineering")
2. **Project Feed** — Infinite-scroll feed of user posts with images, code snippets, and descriptions
3. **User Profiles** — Show case section highlighting user's best projects and vibe style
4. **Post Creation** — Simple form to share projects (title, description, tags, media links)
5. **Engagement Actions** — Like, comment, save/bookmark posts

## Not Now (Post-MVP):
- User accounts/authentication (start with guest mode or simple demo)
- Payments / monetization
- Push notifications
- Admin dashboard
- Third-party integrations (GitHub, Discord, etc.)
- Advanced settings / customization

## Design Posture:
**Calm × Playful × Editorial**

- **Calm**: Clean layout, easy to scan, no clutter
- **Playful**: Vibrant colors, emoji, casual tone, creative energy
- **Editorial**: Magazine-style project cards, featured spots, visual hierarchy

Think: *Behance meets Discord but for AI coders*

## References:

| **Dribbble.com** | Visual-first project cards, tagging system, creator focus |

## Done When:
A person can complete this journey on both phone and desktop:
> "I land on the site → browse a few vibe tribes → pick one → read 3 posts → like/comment on one → see my own post example"
